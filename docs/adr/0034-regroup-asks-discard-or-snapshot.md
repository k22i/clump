# Regrouping with edits asks what to do with them

`r` with a dirty grouping asks: `d` discard the edits, `s` keep them as a snapshot
(`before regroup <time>`) and regroup, `esc` cancel. After a regroup the override keeps only its
snapshots, with no active `grouping`.

Silently discarding a user's edits on a keystroke was the alternative, and a regroup is exactly the
moment a user would lose an afternoon's grouping work. Discarding is still offered, because after a
provider change the old groups may be worthless.
